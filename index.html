About this planner

This page helps Astronomical League members plan visual observing for the League's observing programs. Pick your site, tonight's instrument and the programs you are working on. What's next ranks nearby targets from wherever you are pointing now; Tonight lists everything observable; My plan orders a list from west to east.

Credit. The target lists are the work of the Astronomical League and its observing program coordinators. This is an independent planning aid, not an official League tool. Always read the program's own page for its rules, required log details and how to submit: each program name in the target panel links there.

Data. Positions and some magnitudes for NGC, IC and Messier objects come from OpenNGC (CC-BY-SA 4.0). Magnitudes marked SIMBAD come from the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France; most of those are blue (B) magnitudes, which read fainter than visual.

Rules of thumb used.

  • Limiting magnitude = naked-eye limit - 4 + 5 log10(aperture in mm). Stars are rated Easy with 3 magnitudes to spare, OK with 1, Hard at the limit. Galaxies and nebulae are treated as 1.5 magnitudes harder than their listed magnitude, clusters and planetaries 0.5.
  • "Leaving soon" means the target will drop below your minimum altitude at the start of your observing window within about three weeks.
  • Credits count only programs you ticked, that tonight's setup qualifies for, and that you have not yet marked observed. What qualifies (eyes, binoculars or telescope; by hand or go-to; visual or imaging) is taken from each program's "Quick View of Requirements" grid on its AL page.

Your site, equipment and plan are stored in this browser. Observed marks are stored in this browser too; on the Progress tab you can keep them in a file on your own computer (saved automatically in Chrome and Edge), or download a copy.
